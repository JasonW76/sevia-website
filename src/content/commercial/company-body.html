Why Sevia exists.

Australia has already done the hard part. We’ve taken to rooftop solar like almost nowhere else on earth, and businesses have invested heavily in the equipment that runs them — air-conditioning, refrigeration, ventilation, hot water. The build-out is largely done.

What’s missing is how it’s used. Spare solar is sold back for a few cents, then bought again — expensive — at the evening peak. Equipment runs flat out on dumb timers, whether the building needs it or not. And a single busy half-hour, the lunch rush on a hot day, can set a demand charge a business keeps paying for the rest of the month.

The hardware is all there. The intelligence to run it isn’t.

That’s the gap Sevia exists to close. Not more panels or more boxes on the wall — a brain that forecasts the day, works the equipment you already have, and gets more from every piece of it. A flatter peak, more of your own sun, less waste. Better software, not more hardware.

The founder

A hands-on builder with a delivery record.

Sevia is founded and built by Jason Watson. He’s an environmental scientist with 25 years in the field — including leading the multimillion-dollar regional environment and water engineering business of a Tier 1 consultancy, directing high-performing teams and commercial delivery. His work runs through the renewable build-out itself: leading the environmental assurance on grid-scale projects, from the Clare Solar Farm in Queensland to the Capital Battery here in the ACT.

For years he watched good solar sold back for cents, and equipment running flat out with no thought for the cost or the grid. So he built the fix himself — designing and engineering the energy brain by hand, not commissioning it out. It runs in production today, forecasting, controlling real hardware and scoring its own calls every hour.

Sevia is where that brain goes next: the commercial version, in development now for mid-market venues — restaurants, clubs and pubs — with HVAC and electrical engineers shaping the control design. Behind it is a rare pairing — someone who has run large-scale commercial delivery, and built the system with his own hands.

How we build it

Engineered for real plant.

Controlling real plant is engineering, and we treat it that way. Software that reaches into air-conditioning, kitchen ventilation and refrigeration has to respect how that equipment is meant to run — its safe limits, its sequences, its margins. So we build the commercial product with HVAC and electrical engineers shaping the control design alongside the brain. It’s how you earn the right to act on someone’s building: Sevia works with your equipment, inside its limits, every time.

Where we’re headed

A smarter grid, one building at a time.

Every venue that flattens its peak and runs on its own sun is a building that leans on the grid less when it’s most strained. Do that across thousands of sites and you get what the grid actually needs — not more poles and wires, but better use of what’s already built. That’s the world Sevia is for.
