How it works

It watches, decides, and acts.

It watches

Sevia reads your solar surplus in real time — how much is spare right now, heading to the grid for pennies.

It decides

It works out which device needs that solar most — tank cold? the heat pump wins. Car low? it gets a share. It coordinates so they never fight over the same kilowatt.

It acts

It sends the command and tells you, in plain English, what it did: “Heating on sunshine.” No kilowatt soup, no jargon.

The Mind

It thinks out loud. In plain English.

Sevia isn’t a solar diverter following a timer. Every decision weighs your surplus, your tariff, your deadlines and the day’s forecast — and it tells you the single most important thing it’s doing right now, and why. Not a wall of data. A reason.

It learns your home

It gets smarter the longer it lives with you.

Sevia keeps a quiet record of every decision and what happened, and uses it to tune itself to your home in particular — the same proven approach a smart thermostat like Nest takes. The longer it runs, the sharper and more efficient it gets.

Hot water

Your tank’s real speed

It learns exactly how fast your tank heats and how slowly it cools. So it can leave the heating later and later on solar — hitting your deadline with less and less grid power, and never a cold shower.

Your house

Your home’s thermal fingerprint

It learns how quickly your rooms warm and cool — your home’s own thermal response. Pre-cooling before a hot afternoon gets tuned to how your house actually holds temperature: efficient comfort that coasts through the peak, not a generic guess.

Your car

Your week, understood

It learns your driving pattern — which days the car’s home in the sun, and how much charge you need by when — so it plans surplus charging around your real routine instead of reacting each night.

It also looks ahead, not just back. Sevia reads a solar forecast for your exact rooftop (via Solcast) and your local weather, so it plans the day instead of only reacting to it — holding a device for sun that’s an hour away, pre-heating before a frosty morning peak, pre-cooling ahead of a hot afternoon, planning tomorrow’s car charge on tomorrow’s expected sun. Anticipate, don’t react.

And it keeps the long view. Week by week it tunes; season by season it learns the slow patterns — the cold winter mornings your heat pump has to work harder through, the summer months when surplus is everywhere; and year on year the picture only sharpens. The data compounds — the estimates it starts with quietly become measured truths your bill confirms.

And a rule that never bends: the learning only ever makes the guarantees more efficient — never looser.
