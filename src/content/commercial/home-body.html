The demand charge

One moment sets a big part of your bill.

On a commercial network tariff, your demand charge is set by your single highest 30-minute interval in the month — during business hours. Push the peak up once, when the lunch rush and every unit land together, and you pay for that moment all month. Most operators never see it coming, and cutting energy use alone doesn't fix it.

How it works

It starts with your energy data.

No guesswork, no leap of faith. We start by reading your actual energy data — to see exactly where your peak, and your money, is going. Then we configure Sevia to your site and prove the saving on your own bill.

1

Energy audit

We read your actual energy data — your bill, your demand profile, your loads — and work out where the peak is, what's shiftable, and what Sevia can do about it. No cost, no obligation.

2

Configure & connect

Sevia needs to see and steer your site. We fit a power meter with clamps on your switchboard — it watches your whole-site demand second by second — plus networked controls on your rooftop air-con, an on-site gateway on its own mobile link, and temperature sensors on your cold rooms and freezers. Off-the-shelf hardware, installed by a licensed electrician.

3

Learn & shadow

Before Sevia acts on anything, it learns your site — a minimum of three weeks watching your loads, patterns and peaks, and shadowing its own decisions to prove them out. It starts only once it knows your site.

4

Run, measure & learn

Sevia takes over the day — and never stops learning. Your saving is measured on your own bill, not modelled, not promised.

Sustainability

Use the sun you already paid for.

Most sites export their spare solar for a few cents, then buy expensive grid power back at the peak. Sevia closes that gap — it soaks your surplus into the loads that can hold it, so more of your own solar stays on site, working for you.

Use more of your own solar

Sevia shifts cooling, refrigeration and hot water into your solar hours, and pre-cools before the peak — turning spare sun into value on your site instead of a cheap export.

Waste less of it

Every kilowatt you use on site is one you're not exporting for cents, or buying back at the peak price. Sevia keeps your surplus working instead of leaking away.

Run on your own sunshine

More of your day powered by the solar on your own roof, and less leaning on the grid when it's most strained — getting full value from what you've already built.

The bigger picture

Smaller bills. A smarter grid.

Australia has already built the solar and the equipment — most of it just isn't used well. Sevia gets more out of what's already there: more of your own sunshine used, less waste, a flatter peak. That's also what a cleaner, more reliable grid needs. Better software, not more hardware.
