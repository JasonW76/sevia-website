The technology

Sevia works a day ahead.

Most energy systems only react — the peak forms, they scramble. Sevia runs the other way round: it forecasts the day, plans the cheapest path through it, and acts before the peak arrives — then proves every move on your own meter.

Equipment control

Sevia nudges the gear you already run — easing a degree here, staging a compressor there — always inside safe limits, never forcing it.

  • Networked control of your rooftop a/c

Learning

It learns how your building actually behaves — how fast your cold room recovers, when your rush lands — and gets sharper every week.

  • Shadows your site before it acts

Measurement

Savings are verified on your own bill, to the international IPMVP standard — measured, not modelled.

  • Baseline from your shadow period

  • Verified on your meter

Safety

The worst Sevia can do is use a little more energy — never create a hazard.

  • Fails safe: ventilation full, cooling on
  • The meter only measures; it can't act
  • Never touches cooking or food safety
Foresight

It knows the hot afternoon is coming hours before you feel it, and it has already made room. When a cloud passes, it knows the sun is right behind it — so it holds steady instead of flinching at every flicker.

How it fits together

From your switchboard to the brain, and back.

Sevia reaches the gear you already run over the languages it already speaks — Modbus and BACnet — through one on-site gateway on its own mobile link. Measurements flow up to the brain in the cloud; safe setpoints flow back down. Nothing on your site is exposed to the internet.
